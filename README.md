# Sen Du

**Product Development Director at GeoKinesia** · InSAR · SAR signal processing · Ground deformation

I work across radar remote sensing, deformation monitoring, and geospatial product development. At GeoKinesia, I lead product development and R&D. My research interests include InSAR and MT-InSAR, offset tracking, 3D deformation inversion, uncertainty quantification, and physics-constrained machine learning for geohazards.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Geosciences (IGEO, CSIC-UCM) from 2023 to 2025. Before that, I conducted doctoral research at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC) as a China Scholarship Council fellow.
